Prototype — not live data

A clickable mock of an Enquiry section inside the quote — the mirror of the Freight section. Open the folder, go to the Enquiry tab, and press Create enquiry: it builds a supplier enquiry from the quote's items. You can add / delete rows, split it into two enquiries (e.g. to different suppliers), and then send — choosing who it goes to and where responses land. Suppliers' offers come back as reply threads.
